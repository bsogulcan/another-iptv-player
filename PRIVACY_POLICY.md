# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

## The app

Another IPTV Player is a free, open-source player. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Playlists, provider credentials, favorites, and watch history are stored on your device. Playback and related requests connect to your chosen IPTV provider and services needed to display content.

## Optional support and issue reports

In iOS 3.0.0, you can contact the developer or report an issue from Settings. Nothing is sent automatically. You review and send an email, or choose a destination in the system share sheet if Mail is unavailable.

If you email support, we receive your sender email address and display name, your message, and the app version, iOS version, device model, and report date included in the message. We use these details to respond and resolve your request. Email is processed by your email provider and our receiving email service, Gmail. If you choose another sharing service, its privacy policy applies to that transfer.

Diagnostic attachments are optional and disabled by default. They may contain application events, playback and AirPlay errors, and IPTV API response details. Logs are stored locally in a rotating archive with a seven-day age limit and a size limit. Recognized URLs, authorization values, and credential fields are redacted, but redaction is not a guarantee that every sensitive detail is removed. You can preview logs before sharing; avoid including passwords or private information in your message.

Support emails and attached logs are used for customer support and troubleshooting, not advertising or tracking. We delete them from our support mailbox within 30 days after the request is closed. You can request earlier deletion by emailing bsogulcan@gmail.com. Copies held by your chosen email or sharing provider are governed by that provider's policies.

## Optional tips and purchases

iOS offers optional, one-time consumable tips through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Tips unlock no features and are not subscriptions. Apple processes payment; we do not receive your payment card or bank details. The app uses StoreKit to verify and finish tip transactions and does not send transaction records to a developer-operated purchase server. Apple's privacy policy applies to its payment services.

## Website

The marketing website uses Google Analytics to understand traffic, such as page views and country. This is separate from the app, which has no analytics SDK. You can use a content blocker or Google's opt-out tools to limit website analytics.

## Third-party services

Your IPTV provider processes requests needed to supply your content. Apple handles optional purchases, and email or sharing providers handle reports you choose to send. Their respective privacy policies apply. The app does not automatically upload diagnostic logs to the developer.

## Open source and contact

The source code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/bsogulcan/another-iptv-player). For privacy questions or deletion requests, email bsogulcan@gmail.com. You can also open an issue in the project's GitHub repository; do not post private logs or credentials in public issues.

## Changes

Updates to this policy will be reflected in this document and the website with a revised last-updated date.
